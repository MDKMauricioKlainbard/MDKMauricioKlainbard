## 🧬 About Me

I am an Engineering student focused on solving complex problems from **first principles**. My passion lies at the intersection of **Systems Programming (C++)** and **Theoretical Physics**.

Leveraging my background in Full Stack development, I am shifting my focus toward **Computational Engineering**. I aim to build simulation engines and tools that help visualize the laws of the universe, bridging the gap between abstract mathematical theory and high-performance code.
